# Clientify

**Clientify** is a modern **Client Management System** designed to help businesses and agencies manage clients, projects, tasks, payments, documents, team members, and client relationships from one centralized platform.

The system provides a complete workflow for managing the client lifecycle — from onboarding and project management to task tracking, invoicing, payments, and reporting.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

* 👥 Client Management
* 📁 Project Management
* ✅ Task Management
* 💰 Payment & Invoice Management
* 📄 Document Management
* 📊 Business Dashboard
* 👤 Team & User Management
* 🔐 Role-Based Authentication
* 🔔 Notifications
* 📈 Reports & Analytics
* 📅 Deadline & Activity Tracking
* 🔎 Search & Filtering
